## Problem Statement

Ace Your Course (AYC) is a small Iloilo City company, run by people who passed
their own board exams. It makes physical reviewers (formula flash cards, A3
posters, A5 notebooks and bundles) for Philippine licensure Exams, and sells
them only on Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop.

- **No home of its own.** A reviewee searching "CELE reviewer" finds
  Marketplace listings and copycat shops, not AYC. There is nowhere that shows
  the whole catalogue by Exam, explains which format suits which way of
  studying, collects the honest reviews, answers the common questions
  (shipping, SPayLater, "is this the original?"), or proves the products are
  the genuine, IPOPHIL-registered ones.
- **Nothing digital to sell.** AYC already has the content (thousands of
  formula cards, quizzes and reviewers), but reviewees who study on their
  phones can't buy any of it.
- **Staff can't run anything themselves.** Keeping a site current, publishing
  digital content and handling digital sales all need tools that don't exist
  yet.

## Solution

One Next.js application with three Surfaces:

- **The Website** is the public catalogue and brand home.
  - Every Product AYC sells, organised by Exam, with format guidance, Shopee
    figures, hand-curated Reviews, an FAQ, an About story, an authenticity
    guide and legal pages.
  - It sells nothing itself. Every buy button opens the Product's Marketplace
    Listing.
  - Once the Member App launches, the Website also sends visitors there.
- **The Admin** is the staff-only Surface.
  - Owners and Editors manage Website content without a developer: Products
    and their Listings, Reviews, FAQs, Exam page sections, the promo banner and
    Settings.
  - They also manage Member App content: Reviewer PDFs, flashcards and quizzes
    (by hand or by spreadsheet import), and later Courses.
  - Owners also run digital sales: Plans, Orders, Members and Access Pass
    grants.
- **The Member App** is a signed-in study app.
  - A reviewee signs in with Google, adds the Exams they're preparing for (with
    a countdown to each Exam Date), and studies the Library one Topic at a
    time: flashcards, scored quizzes and watermarked Reviewer PDF pages.
  - It tracks progress, Weak Topics and a Study Streak.
  - Free Preview content lets anyone try it. A paid Plan (for example "CE
    All-Access · 3 months") unlocks all of an Exam's content for a fixed
    period, paid through PayMongo, and never renews by itself.

## User Stories

### Phase 1: Website

**Discovery and home**

1. As a Visitor, I want a Home page that says what AYC is ("Remember every formula on board day") and who made it, so that I know within seconds I'm in the right place.
2. As a Visitor, I want a "Try a card" widget that flips real sample cards, so that I can see the quality before I buy.
3. As a Visitor, I want a "Find your exam" picker on Home with my Exam's Subjects and its Products sorted by units sold, so that I can jump straight to what's relevant.
4. As a Visitor, I want a Best sellers row ranked by units sold on Shopee, so that I can see what other reviewees buy.
5. As a Visitor, I want a "Which format fits how you study?" section comparing flash cards, notebooks, posters and bundles with starting prices, so that I can choose a format.
6. As a Visitor, I want to watch a one-minute video of the products, so that I can see what's inside before I order.
7. As a Visitor on mobile data, I want the hero video not to download until I tap play, so that the page loads fast and doesn't eat my data.
8. As a Visitor, I want a scrolling wall of real Reviews, including the critical ones, so that I trust they're honest.
9. As a Visitor, I want an authenticity section explaining how to spot fake copies, so that I don't buy pirated photocopies.
10. As a Visitor, I want shop-wide trust figures (rating, number of ratings, followers) with an "as of" date, so that I know they're real and current.
11. As a Visitor, I want a dismissible announcement bar that stays dismissed on my next visit, so that it doesn't nag me.
12. As a Visitor, I want a promo banner during sales ("11.11 · Any 2 reviewers, 4% off") that disappears by itself after the sale, so that I hear about deals while they're on.

**Catalogue browsing**

13. As a Visitor, I want a Products page listing every Product for every Exam, so that I can browse the whole catalogue.
14. As a Visitor, I want to filter Products by Exam, by format and by maximum price, and search by name, Exam or Subject, so that I find the right one quickly.
15. As a Visitor, I want to sort Products by best selling, price (both directions) and top rated, so that I can compare.
16. As a Visitor, I want filters reflected in the URL, so that I can share or bookmark a filtered view.
17. As a Visitor, I want a "Help me choose" two-question picker (my Exam, how I study) that recommends one Product with a reason, so that I'm not overwhelmed.
18. As a Visitor, I want an empty state with "Clear filters" when nothing matches, so that I'm never stuck.
19. As a Visitor, I want Coming Soon Products shown in their own row with a "Coming soon" badge and no price, so that I know what's planned without mistaking it for something I can buy.
20. As a Visitor, I want Coming Soon Products left out of counts, sorting, the price filter and the ratings table, so that the numbers I see are about real, buyable Products.

**Product page**

21. As a Visitor, I want a Product page with a photo gallery, Exam, format, size, paper, edition and the Subjects covered (full names), so that I know exactly what I'm getting.
22. As a Visitor, I want to see the variations and their prices (for example "MSTC only ₱400" or "all three ₱1,250"), so that I can buy per Subject on a budget.
23. As a Visitor, I want a price range shown when a Product has variations, so that I know the cheapest way in.
24. As a Visitor, I want buy buttons for Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop that open this Product's own Listing (or the shop page if it has none), so that I can buy on my preferred Marketplace.
25. As a Visitor, I want a deal badge ("Shopee: any 2 enjoy 4% off") on Products that have the deal, so that I can save.
26. As a Visitor, I want all of the Product's Reviews and its Shopee rating figures on the page, so that I can judge it.
27. As a Visitor, I want "More for the {board}" suggestions, so that I can complete my set.
28. As a Visitor, I want a friendly message on Products with no written Reviews yet, so that a new Product doesn't look broken.

**Exam pages**

29. As a Visitor, I want an Exam page for every Exam AYC sells for (CE, ME, EE, MT, CPA, LET, PhLE and the rest), so that I land on AYC when I search for my board's reviewer.
30. As a Visitor, I want the Exam page to show its board, long name, blurb, Subjects with full names, and computed stats (number of Products, units sold, average rating, starting price), so that I see AYC's coverage at a glance.
31. As a Visitor, I want every Product for the Exam, best selling first, with format filter chips and a "Which one should I get?" comparison table, so that I can choose.
32. As a Visitor, I want three sample formula cards for my Exam, so that I can see what the content looks like.
33. As a Visitor, I want the CE bundle offer on the CE page, so that I see the best-value option.
34. As a Visitor, I want Reviews from reviewees of my Exam, so that the social proof is relevant to me.
35. As a Visitor, I want switching Exam on the Exam page to change the URL, so that I can share the right page.

**Reviews, FAQ, About, Legal**

36. As a Visitor, I want a Reviews page with ratings per Product, a searchable wall, star and Exam filters, and sorting (most helpful, highest, most critical, most detailed), so that I can find reviews that address my concerns.
37. As a Visitor, I want to filter the review wall by one Product from the ratings table, so that I can read about the exact Product I'm considering.
38. As a Visitor, I want an FAQ grouped by topic, with search, popular questions and expand or collapse all, so that I get answers without messaging the shop.
39. As a Visitor, I want some FAQ answers backed by a quote from a real Review, so that the answers feel credible.
40. As a Visitor, I want an About page with AYC's story, mission and Iloilo roots, so that I trust the people behind the products.
41. As a Visitor, I want a Privacy Policy and Terms of Use that describe what the Website actually does, so that I know how my data and purchases are handled.
42. As a Visitor, I want one consistent contact email everywhere, so that I know how to reach AYC.

**Navigation, SEO and analytics**

43. As a Visitor, I want an Exams menu listing every Exam, and a footer with Marketplace, Exam, shop and social links, so that I can get anywhere from any page.
44. As a Visitor on a phone, I want a mobile menu and layouts that fit small screens, so that the Website is easy to use where I actually browse.
45. As a Visitor, I want the Website to work without asking me to accept cookies, so that I'm not interrupted.
46. As a Visitor searching Google, I want AYC's pages to have clear titles, descriptions and share images, so that I recognise them in results and in links friends send me.
47. As a Visitor, I want accessible pages (headings, contrast, keyboard use, alt text), so that I can use the Website with assistive technology.
48. As an Owner, I want to know how many Visitors click through to each Marketplace, from which Product and page, so that I can tell whether the Website drives sales.
49. As an Owner, I want Member App buttons, badges and sections hidden until I switch the Member App on, so that Visitors never hit a dead end before it launches.
50. As a Visitor, once the Member App is live, I want an "ALSO IN APP" badge on Products whose content is in the Member App, with a "See plans" link, so that I know I can study it on my phone.

### Phase 2: Admin (Website part)

**Access and Staff**

51. As Staff, I want to sign in to the Admin with my Google account, so that I don't manage another password.
52. As a signed-in User who isn't Staff, I want a clear "not authorised" screen that offers to switch account or go to the Member App, so that I know what happened.
53. As an Owner, I want failed Admin sign-in attempts recorded, so that I can spot misuse.
54. As an Owner, I want to invite a person by their Google email with a Staff Role (Owner or Editor), and copy an invite message to send them myself, so that I can add teammates.
55. As an invited person, I want my invite to activate the first time I sign in with that Google account, so that joining takes one step.
56. As an Owner, I want invites to expire after 7 days, and to renew or cancel them, so that stale invites don't linger.
57. As an Owner, I want to remove Staff or change their Staff Role, and have removal take effect immediately, so that access stays correct.
58. As an Owner, I want the system to stop me removing or demoting the last Owner, so that AYC is never locked out.
59. As an Editor, I want to see only the sections I'm allowed to use, so that the Admin isn't confusing.
60. As an Owner, I want Editors blocked from Plans, Orders, Users and Settings on the server, not just hidden in the menu, so that permissions can't be bypassed.

**Overview**

**Products**

63. As an Editor, I want a Products list with search, Exam filter, a "missing Listings" filter, and S/L/T badges showing which Marketplaces have a Listing, so that I can keep links complete.
64. As an Editor, I want to add and edit a Product: name, short name, edition, Exam, format, size and paper, Subjects (from that Exam's Subjects), price from and up to, variations, photos (first is the cover; drag to reorder), product page text, Listings per Marketplace, and SEO title and description, so that the Website shows accurate information.
65. As an Editor, I want to enter a Product's Shopee rating, number of ratings and units sold, with the "as of" date set when I save, so that best-seller rankings stay current.
66. As an Editor, I want to mark a Product as Coming Soon, Deal, "Available in app" and "Show on website", so that I control how it appears.
67. As an Editor, I want deleting a Product to need typed confirmation and to explain that Marketplace Listings aren't affected, so that I don't delete by accident.
68. As an Editor, I want my saves to appear on the Website within seconds, so that fixes are immediate.
69. As an Editor, I want a clear error with my edits kept when a save fails, so that I don't lose work.

**Reviews and FAQs**

70. As an Editor, I want to add a Review by pasting its text word for word, with stars, Product, variation, Marketplace and helpful votes, and a live preview, so that the Website shows genuine Reviews.
71. As an Editor, I want to pin Reviews to Home, with a warning when more than 9 are pinned (Home shows the first 9), so that Home shows the best ones.
72. As an Editor, I want to filter Reviews by Exam, stars and pinned status, and search their text, so that I can manage many.
73. As an Editor, I want to add, edit, hide, reorder and delete FAQ questions within the fixed groups, with a 400-character answer limit and an optional supporting Review quote, so that the FAQ stays short and credible.

**Exam pages, promo and Settings**

74. As an Editor, I want to show or hide optional sections on each Exam page and edit their text, so that each page fits its Exam.
75. As an Editor, I want to edit each Exam page's SEO title, description and share image, with a search preview and length guidance, so that it ranks and shares well.
76. As an Editor, I want to set the Home promo banner's text, button, link, start date and end date, so that it appears and disappears with the sale without my intervention.
77. As an Editor, I want to set each Exam's Official Exam Date from the PRC schedule, so that the Member App countdowns start correctly.
78. As an Owner, I want to edit the contact email and the Marketplace shop and social links in Settings, with validation, so that every button and the footer update at once.
79. As an Owner, I want to update the shop-wide trust figures with an "as of" date, so that the Website's trust line stays honest.
80. As an Owner, I want every Admin change recorded (who, what, when, before and after), so that mistakes can be traced and undone.
    80a. As an Editor, I want to edit the search title, description and share image of Home, Products, Reviews, About and FAQ, with the same search preview and checks as Exam pages, so that every Site page ranks and shares well. (Added 2026-11-05.)

## Implementation Decisions

### Architecture and cross-cutting

- **One app, three Surfaces, one Supabase project** (ADR 0008): the Website
  at the root, the Member App under `/app`, the Admin under `/admin`. Hosted on
  Vercel Pro (Singapore) with Supabase Pro (Singapore) (ADR 0012).
- **No tenancy** (ADR 0008, supersedes 0005). Every User is a Member. Staff
  is a database staff list keyed by email, with Staff Role `owner` or `editor`
  and statuses invited, active and removed (invites expire after 7 days).
  The server makes every access decision (the Staff Guard, and from Phase 3
  the Member's own records) and connects through Drizzle with full access.
  Every table has RLS on with no `anon` or `authenticated` policies, so the
  browser's publishable key reads and writes nothing (decided in Phase 2).
  The hosted project serves development and production. Tests never touch it:
  the database tests run in CI against a local Supabase (Phase 2:
  Environments).
- **Template cleanup.** These follow from ADR 0008 and Google-only sign-in.
  - Remove the template's tenancy: Organizations, Memberships, the workspace
    switcher, the Org Role and Superadmin role model, and the tenancy
    migration and policies.
  - Remove the email and password auth flows: sign-up, confirm, reset and
    update password.
  - Remove the template dashboard demo screens (Customers, Team, Role
    settings, Blank) and the theme toggle.
  - The **Catalogue seam becomes the new Reference Feature**. Agent docs and
    the glossary are updated to point to it before Customers is removed.
- **Authentication is Google OAuth only**, through Supabase, for both the
  Member App and the Admin. Authorization decisions are made on the server
  from the staff list and the Member's own records, never from
  client-settable metadata.
- **Service Seam everywhere** (ADR 0003 and 0006). Every Surface reads and
  writes through typed service modules. Reads happen in Server Components and
  writes in Server Actions validated with zod. Each service exposes a typed
  contract that the mock and database implementations both satisfy, as the
  Customers seam does.
- **Routes:** `/`, `/products` (query filters `exam`, `format`, `q`, `sort`,
  `max`), `/products/[slug]`, `/exams/[code]` (lowercase code), `/reviews`,
  `/faq`, `/about`, `/legal/privacy`, `/legal/terms`, `/app/...` and
  `/admin/...`. Every path is defined in the central paths module.
- **Identity:** a Product has its own ID and a readable slug. Marketplace
  item IDs are attributes of its Marketplace Listings and never the Product's
  identity.
- **Site URL and contact email are configuration.** The domain isn't
  transferred yet, so canonical URLs, the sitemap and share links are built
  from one site-URL setting. The public contact (support and data protection)
  is `aceyourcourseinc@gmail.com`: a config value in Phase 1, an Owner Setting
  from Phase 2.
- **Design system.** AYC brand tokens in Tailwind and shadcn: cream `#F6F1E7`,
  navy `#0E1A2E`, orange `#FF881F` and blue `#005AA5`, hard offset shadows and
  borders, and Schibsted Grotesk with JetBrains Mono. All Surfaces use these
  tokens and are light-only. The Website and Member App closely follow the
  mockup's layouts and copy. So does the Admin: it follows its mockup's
  layouts, copy and flows at production quality, and where the mockup's copy
  conflicts with a decision in this spec, the decision wins (decided in
  Phase 2).
- **Time zone.** All day boundaries (Study Day, the countdown, Access Pass end
  display, promo banner dates) use Asia/Manila.
- **Money** is stored in centavos as integers and displayed in ₱ with en-PH
  formatting.

### Phase 1: Website

- **Catalogue seam (mock-first).** Port the mockup's data into a typed seed
  behind a Catalogue service. Its contract covers:
  - Exams: list them; get one by code, with its Subjects
  - Products: list them with filter and sort options; get one by slug;
    related Products; best sellers
  - Reviews: list with filters and sorting; the Home selection
  - FAQs and shop stats
  - Website settings: Marketplace shop links, social links, contact, the
    promo banner and the announcement bar
- **Seed data.** The seed is replaced by the client's full catalogue
  spreadsheet (about 29 Products across about 11 Exams) when it arrives.
  Building doesn't wait for it, but launch does.
- **Product model.** It carries:
  - Exam, format (Flash cards, Notebook, Posters or Bundle), size and paper,
    edition, and Subjects
  - price from and optional price up to; variations, each with a name and price
  - photos in order, with the first as the cover
  - one Listing per Marketplace (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop)
  - Shopee figures (rating, number of ratings, units sold) with an "as of"
    date
  - flags: Deal, "Available in app", "Show on website"
  - a lifecycle state, **Live** or **Coming Soon**
  - product page text ("what's inside", "made for"), with generated defaults
  - SEO title and description, with generated defaults
- **Coming Soon rules.**
  - It shows a badge and no price, and has no buy buttons apart from an
    optional "Follow on Shopee" link to the shop page.
  - It is excluded from counts, sorting, the price filter, the picker and the
    ratings table.
  - It appears only on its Exam page and in a "Coming soon" row on Products.
  - The mockup's "₱0" display must never appear.
- **Store buttons.** Use the Product's own Listing for that Marketplace,
  otherwise the Marketplace's shop page from Settings. An empty shop page
  hides that button. The selected variation is not passed to the Marketplace.
  No stock labels are shown, because the Marketplaces own stock.
- **Shopee figures and Reviews are entered by hand,** shown with an "as of"
  date. There is no Marketplace API and no scraping. Reviews are curated and
  copied word for word. They are displayed, but **never** emitted as Review
  or AggregateRating structured data (Google's self-serving and third-party
  review rules).
- **Fixed copy lives in typed content config:** the ticker, the 4 steps, the
  format cards, the best-for map, 3 sample cards per Exam, the announcement
  bar messages and the promo banner (with start and end dates). The About
  stats (number of Products and Exams) are computed from the catalogue.
- **Media.**
  - Product photos are self-hosted (repo assets in Phase 1, Supabase Storage
    from Phase 2) and served through the image optimizer. Shopee's CDN is
    never hot-linked.
  - The promo video is a self-hosted compressed version (about 2–4 MB,
    720p). On mobile, only the poster shows until the Visitor taps play. The
    `#video` section uses the same file.
- **Member App switch.** A single "Member App live" switch, off at launch.
  While it's off, the Website hides every Member App button and link (nav
  "Sign in" and "Start reviewing", "Try the app free", "See app plans",
  "Review {Exam} in the app", "Open the app"), the ALSO IN APP badges and
  "Included in All-Access" band, the "Physical or app?" section, the Home app
  band and the picker's App option. It flips with no code change.
- **SEO.**
  - Per-page title, description and share image.
  - A sitemap of every Exam and Product page plus the static pages, and a
    robots file.
  - JSON-LD for Organization (with sameAs links to the Marketplaces and
    socials), BreadcrumbList (Product and Exam pages), Product with Offers
    pointing at the Listings and **no rating markup**, and FAQPage.
- **Analytics** (ADR 0009). PostHog runs cookieless and anonymous (nothing
  stored on the device, IP discarded, no person profiles) with no consent
  banner. Events: pageviews, and `marketplace_click` with Product,
  Marketplace and page.
- **Legal.** Website-only Privacy Policy and Terms v1.0, versioned in the
  repo with an effective date. The Member App sections arrive as v2.0 at
  Member App launch.
- **Mockup fixes:**
  - the footer "Reviews" link goes to the Reviews page
  - the Exam switcher updates the URL
  - format cards link to Products pre-filtered by format
  - the About call-to-action lists every Exam
  - the announcement bar's dismissal is remembered in the browser
  - "Most are also in the app"-style claims are computed, not hardcoded
- **Responsive:** mobile-first, using the mockup's 760 px breakpoint as the
  main layout switch.
- **Rendering and caching.** The strict CSP uses a per-request nonce, so every
  page renders per request and none are pre-built as static pages. Keep the
  nonce CSP. Speed comes from caching at the Catalogue seam instead: its reads
  are cached and tagged (for example by Product, by Exam, and for Reviews,
  FAQs and settings). In Phase 1 the seed never changes at runtime. From
  Phase 2, an Admin save invalidates the affected tags.
- **Middleware scope.** Website routes need no session. The middleware still
  sets the CSP on every route, but it calls Supabase only for the Member App,
  the Admin and the auth callback, so public pages make no Supabase round trip.

### Phase 2: Admin (Website part)

Grilled on 2026-11-01. The Admin is built in two stages. First, every screen
and rule works on editable sample data. Then the database goes in behind the
same seams.

- **Mockup fidelity.** The Admin closely follows the Admin mockup (v2) in its
  layouts, copy, look and flows, built to production quality:
  - real validation
  - loading, empty and error states
  - mobile, with the slide-in menu drawer
  - accessibility

  Where the mockup's copy conflicts with a decision here, the decision wins and
  only that copy changes. For example, invites are shared by hand, not
  emailed, and the mockup's "course" (meaning an Exam) is "Exam".

- **Menu.** Overview · Pages & promo · Products & store links · Reviews &
  FAQs · Settings (Owner only) · "View site ↗". "Pages & promo" was the
  mockup's "Exam pages & promo", renamed on 2026-11-05 when it took the Site
  pages. No section from a later phase
  appears, greyed out or otherwise. The sidebar's "Paid today" card and the
  Overview's sales tiles, chart and member panels come with Phase 4.
- **Two stages.**
  - **Stage A, sample data.**
    - Every Admin function works on an editable in-memory copy of the Phase 1
      seed, held by the dev server.
    - The header keeps the mockup's "SAMPLE DATA" tag, with a Reset button.
      Restarting the server also resets the data.
    - "Continue with Google" opens a development-only "Sign in as" list of
      sample Users: an Owner, an Editor, an invited email, an expired invite,
      and someone who isn't on the staff list.
    - A production build never offers that list. Without the database, the
      Admin stays closed.
  - **Stage B, the database.** Each seam gets a database arm, chosen when a
    database URL is configured. The screens don't change, and the same
    behaviour tests run against both arms.
- **Seams.**
  - **Catalogue seam.** The Website's reads. The contract is unchanged, and
    the reads are now cached with tags (per Product, per Exam, Reviews, FAQs,
    settings and Exam pages).
  - **Admin seam (new).** Every Admin read and write; reads are always fresh.
    A write:
    - takes the acting Staff User and the record's last-saved stamp
    - validates its input with zod
    - runs the conflict check
    - applies the change and appends Staff Activity in the same step (a
      transaction in Stage B)
    - returns the cache tags to clear, which the calling Server Action then
      clears
  - **Staff Guard (new).** It resolves the current Staff User and Staff Role,
    and every Admin page and every Admin Server Action calls it. Stage A uses
    the "Sign in as" arm; Stage B uses Supabase Google sign-in.
  - **Image upload (new).**
    - A Staff-checked Server Action hands the browser a one-time upload URL.
    - The browser uploads the file to it directly, which avoids the request
      size limits on Server Actions and Vercel functions.
    - The save then records the file's path.
    - Stage A uses a development-only upload route held in memory. Stage B
      uses a Supabase Storage signed upload URL into a public bucket.
- **Environments.** One hosted Supabase project serves development (`pnpm dev`
  on the developer's machine, with no Docker) and production. No test touches
  it. `pnpm test` runs on the mock, and e2e runs locally on the in-memory
  sample data. The database tests and the live-mode Admin e2e run only in CI,
  against a throwaway local Supabase on the runner. Until launch the hosted
  data is working data. After launch, every Admin save from localhost is a
  live edit. Revised 2026-11-04; this replaces "local Supabase for
  development and every test".
- **Development password sign-in, until go-live.** Added 2026-11-04.
  - Under `pnpm dev` in live mode, while the Supabase project has its Email
    provider on, the sign-in screen shows a "DEVELOPMENT ONLY" email and
    password box under "Continue with Google". It only signs in: there's no
    sign-up and no password reset.
  - Its Users are made by hand in the Supabase dashboard, already confirmed:
    the developer as the Owner, and plus-addresses invited as test Editors.
    In development a password User stands in for a Google account, as the
    sample Users do, and an Invite activates on its confirmed email.
  - Outside `pnpm dev`, the Staff Guard counts a session only when Supabase
    records it as a Google (OAuth) sign-in. So production and previews refuse
    a password session, even one with a confirmed email.
  - While Email is on, Supabase's "Confirm email" stays on. Sign-ups can't be
    closed for Email alone, because a first Google sign-in is a sign-up too.
  - At go-live the test Editors are removed and Email is turned off, which
    ends every password sign-in. Production stays Google-only (story 51).
- **Password sign-in in production, while testing.** Added 2026-11-11
  (ticket 24). Temporary: removed once testing on the deployed site is done.
  - The server-only switch `ADMIN_PASSWORD_SIGN_IN` ("true" or "false",
    unset means "false") lets the Admin show the same email and password box
    and count password sessions outside `pnpm dev`. It's set per environment
    in Vercel, so turning it off needs no code change.
  - `pnpm dev` in live mode behaves as before, with or without the switch.
  - Everything else stays: Staff only (the Staff Guard and the staff list
    decide), no sign-up, no password reset, Users made by hand in the
    Supabase dashboard, "Confirm email" on, Google still offered.
  - The box needs the Supabase Email provider on. Turning Email off, or the
    switch off, ends password sign-in everywhere.
- **Access is checked on the server.** Drizzle connects with full access, and
  the Staff Guard decides who may do what. Every table has RLS on, with no
  policies for `anon` or `authenticated`, so the browser's publishable key can
  read and write nothing. This replaces "enforced by route Guards and RLS".
- **Keys.** `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` is renamed
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, with no fallback. A server-only
  `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY` is used for Storage only.
- **Migrations.**
  - The Drizzle schema is the source of truth. drizzle-kit generates into the
    Supabase migrations folder, with Supabase timestamp prefixes, and
    hand-written SQL (the bucket, the revokes) sits beside it.
  - Apply them with `pnpm db:reset` locally and `supabase db push` in
    production.
  - The separate Drizzle migrations folder and `db:migrate` go away.
  - Local Postgres matches the hosted major version.
- **Catalogue goes real.**
  - Tables: Exams, Subjects, Products, variations, photos, Listings, Reviews,
    FAQs, Exam page settings, the promo banner, the announcement, Website
    settings and shop stats, Staff, Invites, denied sign-ins and Staff
    Activity.
  - `pnpm catalogue:seed` loads the Phase 1 seed, with the photos uploaded
    into the bucket. It runs on every local `db:reset`, and once in
    production, where it refuses if data already exists.
  - After that first load, the Admin is the only way to edit. There is no
    re-import or sync from the spreadsheet.
- **Staff access.**
  - A signed-out request to `/admin` shows the sign-in screen: "Sign in to
    manage the site" and "Continue with Google".
  - A signed-in User who isn't Staff gets the 403 screen ("…isn't on the
    admin list. Nothing was changed."). It offers "Use another account", and
    "Go to the website", which becomes "Go to the member app" once the Member
    App switch is on.
  - The attempt is recorded (email, time and reason), and the 403 screen
    shows its time. There's no list of denied attempts until Phase 4.
  - The first Owner is added with `pnpm staff:add-owner <email>` at
    deployment. No email address goes in the repo.
  - The staff list is read on every Admin request and action, so a removed or
    demoted Staff User loses that access on their next request. Their sign-in
    session isn't ended, because it may also be their Member App session.
- **Invites.**
  - An Invite is a staff-list entry with no token: a Google email, a Staff
    Role, when it was made, and an expiry 7 days after it was made or last
    renewed.
  - It becomes active on the first sign-in whose verified Google email
    matches it, compared case-insensitively.
  - The Owner shares the Admin address by hand with "Copy invite message". No

### Phase 3: Member App study core

- **Content model.**
  - Exam → Subject → Topic.
  - A Topic has a flashcard deck, a quiz and a Reviewer PDF, each optional.
    - A deck is a list of cards, each with front text, a back in formula
      notation, an optional back image and an optional note.
    - A quiz is a list of questions, each with formula-notation text, four
      choices, the correct choice, an explanation and an optional figure,
      plus a passing score (default 70%), shuffle and "show answers after"
      settings.
    - A Reviewer PDF is an ordered set of page images, with its original file
      kept privately.
  - Decks and quizzes are Hidden or Visible. Reviewer PDFs go Processing →
    Draft → Published or Unpublished, or Processing → Failed.
  - Each deck, quiz and Reviewer PDF can be flagged Free Preview.
- **Formula notation:** LaTeX-style source rendered with KaTeX (or an
  equivalent) in both the Member App and the Admin's live preview.
- **Access decision seam** (ADR 0010 and 0011). One server-side function
  decides whether a Member may open a piece of content. It is called by every
  protected read: deck, quiz questions, Reviewer PDF page image, and later
  video. The rule:

  ```
  allowed(member, content, device) =
       memberAppOpen(member)                         // switch on, or Staff, or Beta Tester
    && device is member's Active Device
    && content is Visible / Published                // a Hidden Course stays open to its owners (2026-11-28)
    && ( content.freePreview
      || member is active Staff                     // ADR 0010 amendment, 2026-11-17
      || (!content.course?.soldSeparately
          && activeAccessPass(member, content.exam)) // end > now (Asia/Manila); never a sold-separately Course (2026-11-28)
      || (content.course?.soldSeparately && owns(member, content.course)) )
  ```

  Changed on 2026-11-28 (Phase 5 planning decisions): an Access Pass never
  opens a separately sold Course, and a Hidden Course stays available to the
  Members who own it. An Unpublished Lesson is unavailable to everyone.

  Its outcomes are allowed, locked (show the unlock prompt), expired (show
  renew, progress kept), device-replaced (show Session ended), no device
  token (show "Use the Member App on this device"), unavailable (content
  Hidden or Unpublished: "no longer available", progress kept) and not open
  yet (the beta gate). The beta gate, Staff and device lines were added on
  2026-11-17.

- **Active Device** (ADR 0011).
  - A custom record per Member. A Member App sign-in sets it.
  - Every protected request compares the request's device token with it.
  - Each switch is logged with its time and a device label from the user
    agent (for example "Chrome on Android").
  - Admin sessions are exempt. Supabase's single-session setting is not used.
- **Burned-in watermark.** Reviewer PDF pages are only ever served through a
  protected page endpoint. It runs the access decision, then stamps the
  Member's email and short Member ID into the page image on every request.
  Only the Member's own browser caches it (private, short max-age). No CDN or
  Storage copy is kept. (Changed on 2026-11-17 from "caches the stamped image
  per Member and page": stored copies would grow with Members × pages.)
  Original PDFs and clean page images are never sent to clients. Cards and quizzes get an overlay watermark and no
  text selection.
- **PDF processing** is a background job (ADR 0012).
  1. The Admin uploads straight to private storage, up to 200 MB.
  2. A job renders the page images and records its progress, which the Admin
     shows.
  3. It ends as a Draft, or as Failed with a reason (password-protected,
     corrupt, too large).
  4. Deleting a Reviewer PDF removes its original and its page images.
- **Spreadsheet import** (cards and quizzes).
  - It is a pure validator and planner. It turns the uploaded CSV (plus an
    images zip) into a preview of the Topics to create, the cards or
    questions to add, replace or keep, and row-level errors: a missing field,
    a formula that doesn't render, a missing image, an invalid correct-answer
    letter.
  - Nothing is saved until Staff confirm.
  - Imported content arrives Hidden. Topics are created from the `topic`
    column.
  - Re-importing offers Replace or Add. Replace keeps Card Marks on cards
    whose front text is unchanged.
  - Downloadable templates are provided.
- **Target Exams.** A Member has any number of them (Exam plus Exam Date).
  The Exam Date defaults to the Exam's Official Exam Date and follows Staff
  updates until the Member edits it. The dashboard is per selected Target
  Exam. Exam pills in onboarding are selectable (a mockup bug).
- **Progress calculator** (pure functions over study records; they're stored
  as current state, not an event log, see the 2026-11-17 decisions). Events are
  Card Marked, Quiz Attempt Submitted, Reviewer Page Viewed and, in Phase 5,
  Lesson Progress.
  - **Card Mark:** got or again; the latest wins.
  - **Quiz:** Best Score per quiz. The "quiz average" is the mean of Best
    Scores across the quizzes taken.
  - **Topic %:** the mean of the components present: share of cards marked
    got, Best Score %, share of Reviewer PDF pages viewed.
  - **Subject %:** the mean of its Topic %.
  - **Weak Topic:** Best Score below the passing score, or 5 or more cards
    marked again. The 3 weakest show, lowest first.
  - **Study Day:** any event on an Asia/Manila calendar day.
  - **Study Streak:** consecutive Study Days, current and best.
  - Progress is stored per Member, never deleted when access ends, and
    counted on Free Preview content too.
- **Resume state:** the last deck position, the last Reviewer PDF page (with
  zoom) and, later, the Lesson position are stored per Member.
- **Consent.**
  - Two required checkboxes, unchecked by default; "Agree" is disabled until
    both are ticked.
  - The agreed Terms and Privacy versions and a timestamp are stored.
  - A version change re-prompts the Member.
  - Legal v2.0 (the Member App sections) ships with this phase's public
    launch.
- **Privacy Requests** (data copy and deletion) are records Owners fulfil by
  hand at launch: a copy within 7 days, deletion within 30. Deletion revokes
  Access Passes with no refund, as the confirmation screen says.
- **Beta access.** The Owner-only Users list, the Member detail page, "Grant
  Access Pass" (Exam, days, reason) and "Extend access" (3, 7, 14 or 30 days)
  ship in Phase 3, all logged to Staff Activity. Members get an in-app notice;
  email waits until a sending domain exists.
- **Reviewer PDF reader:** one page at a time, zoom 75–150% in 25% steps, no
  offline caching, load-error retry, and an empty-Topic state.
- **The mockup's hardcoded CE labels** (sidebar badge, dashboard header,
  countdown label, Library heading) become per Target Exam. The unused
  mobile tab-bar code from v1 of the mockup is not built. Mobile uses the
  Menu button and drawer.

#### Phase 3 planning decisions (added 2026-11-17)

**Problem.** Phase 3 has to put the Member App in front of real people, the
Beta Testers, before Payments exist. Staff also need to load thousands of
cards, quizzes and large Reviewer PDFs without a developer. The decisions
above left open who may enter before launch, how the heavy jobs run on the
chosen hosting, and several edges: devices, Topics, corrections and privacy
requests.

**Solution.** A closed beta behind the Member App switch, content tools that
tolerate corrections without costing Members progress, and server mechanics
(the PDF job, watermark stamping, server-side quiz grading) that fit Vercel
and Supabase as chosen in ADR 0012. Grilled and decided with the user on
2026-11-17.

- **How the work is staged: vertical slices.** Each ticket lands its own
  tables, migration, database arm, sample-data arm, screens and tests. e2e
  runs on the in-memory sample data. Database suites run in CI only, and
  executers never connect to the hosted project. The user applies each
  migration to hosted after merging.
- **The beta gate.**
  - While the Member App switch is off, /app admits only active Staff and
    Beta Testers. Everyone else is signed in but sees "Not open yet", and no
    Member records are created for them beyond what sign-in needs. Once the
    switch is on, anyone can join.
  - Beta Testers are a list of Google email addresses, kept by Owners in a
    "Beta Testers" tab of the Owner-only Users screen. They can be added
    before the person has an account, and matching is by verified email,
    like an Invite but with no expiry and no role.
  - Removing a tester blocks them at their next request while the switch is
    off. Their data is kept.
  - Adding and removing testers is logged in Staff Activity.
  - Adding a tester grants nothing. After their first sign-in an Owner grants
    an Access Pass from Users, and until then the tester has Free Preview.
- **Legal for the beta.** Phase 3 drafts Legal v2.0 (the Member App
  sections: account, study data, Active Device and device log, watermarking,
  Privacy Requests and deletion), marked "pending legal review". Testers
  agree to it on the consent screen. The lawyer-approved text gets a new
  version, and the re-consent mechanism asks everyone again.
- **The Active Device: no device token.** A valid session without a device
  token, for example after signing in at /admin, shows a one-tap "Use the
  Member App on this device" screen. Confirming makes this device Active and
  logs the switch, and the old device gets "Session ended". The token is a
  random httpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax cookie, and only its hash is stored.
  Staff are subject to the Active Device in /app like any Member.
- **Staff in the Member App** (ADR 0010 amendment). Active Staff open every
  Exam's Visible or Published content without an Access Pass. Hidden and
  Draft content stays Admin-only.
- **Target Exams.**
  - A Member can remove a Target Exam from Account, with a confirmation, but
    never their last one. Its progress and Access Pass stay, and adding it
    back restores them.
  - A grant for an Exam the Member doesn't have adds it to their Target Exams
    with the Official Exam Date, including one they removed earlier. The
    notice reads "You now have {Exam} All-Access until {date}".
- **Grants and ending access** (Owner only, each logged with a reason).
  - Grant: 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, or a custom 1–365.
  - Extend: 3, 7, 14 or 30 days.
  - "End access now": sets the end to now and keeps progress. The Member
    sees the expired state. From Phase 4 it can end only granted days, never
    paid ones, which refunds handle.
- **In-app notices** (grants, extensions, end of access) are one dismissible
  banner per notice. There's no notification centre.
- **Topics editor.**
  - Each Subject has a Topics list in the Admin. Editors can add, rename,
    move up or down, and delete only when the Topic is empty.
  - A Topic is made three ways: by an import (in order of first appearance
    in the CSV, after the existing Topics), by "+ New topic" while placing a
    Reviewer PDF, or by hand.
  - Progress is tied to the Topic, never its name. An import matches Topics
    by name, so importing under an old name after a rename creates a new
    Topic, and the preview shows that.
  - Reviewers, decks and quizzes block deleting their Exam or Subject
    (already noted under Phase 2).
- **Formula notation in every text field.** Card front, back and note, and
  quiz question, choices and explanation, are plain text with maths in
  `$...$` (inline) or `$$...$$` (its own line). A literal dollar sign is
  written `\$`. A formula the renderer can't parse is a row-level import
  error naming the row and column. The Admin's live preview uses the
  Member App's renderer.
- **Card back images and quiz figures** live in a private bucket. The server
  hands out signed URLs that expire after about 10 minutes, only as part of
  an allowed deck or quiz read. They aren't stamped, and the overlay
  watermark covers them on screen.
- **Quizzes are graded on the server.** Questions are sent without the
  correct choice or explanation. Submitting returns the score and, if "show
  answers after" is on, each missed question's correct answer and
  explanation. With it off, the result lists only the score and which
  questions were missed.
- **Corrections never cost progress.**
  - Quiz Replace keeps every Member's Best Score and attempt history. In an
    old attempt, a removed question reads "This question has since been
    removed". Retakes use the current questions.
  - Deck Replace keeps Card Marks on cards whose front text is unchanged, as
    above.
  - Content hidden or unpublished while a Member is on it shows "no longer
    available" on the next request, with progress kept.
  - Hidden or Draft content never counts in progress denominators.
- **Progress storage** is current state, not an event log: the latest Card
  Mark per Member and card, every Quiz Attempt (score and answers), Reviewer
  pages viewed per Member, and Study Days (Member plus Manila date). The
  pure progress calculator reads these. Replaying every flip isn't supported.
- **Resume** covers the last deck position and the last Reviewer PDF page and
  zoom. Lessons are added in Phase 5.
- **The Reviewer PDF job** (ADR 0012).
  1. The Editor's browser uploads the PDF straight to a private Supabase
     Storage bucket with a resumable upload (Vercel's request-body limit is
     about 4.5 MB), up to 200 MB.
  2. A job row in Postgres records the status and pages done.
  3. A Vercel function renders about 25 pages per run with pdf.js on a
     server-side canvas (permissive licences only; no AGPL MuPDF), saves
     them, updates the job, and starts the next run.
  4. A Vercel Cron every minute restarts any job whose heartbeat has gone
     stale.
  5. Pages are rendered once at about 1600 px wide as WebP, and the reader
     zooms them in the browser.
  6. Failure reasons: password-protected, corrupt, over 200 MB, or too many
     restarts. Each comes with the fix to try.
  7. The Editor can leave the page, and the Admin shows the progress.
- **Watermark stamping** happens on every page request with sharp, after the
  access decision (see "Burned-in watermark" above).
- **Privacy Requests in the Admin** (Owner only).
  - Data copy: "Download this Member's data" produces a JSON file of the
    profile, Target Exams, consents, Access Passes and grants, progress
    records and device log. The Owner emails it from the support inbox.
  - Deletion: "Delete this account" with a typed confirmation removes the
    Supabase user and all their Member data and revokes Access Passes, with
    no refund. Staff Activity records that it happened, without the personal
    data.
  - Deleting is refused while the person is on the staff list. The last
    Owner can never be deleted.
  - Both actions offer "Mark done". Nothing is emailed automatically.
- **Member App analytics** is the same cookieless, anonymous, PageView-only
  PostHog setup as the Website (ADR 0009), with no person profiles and no
  Member ID. Legal v2.0 needs no analytics consent line.
- **Password sign-in in the Member App, while testing.** Added 2026-11-20
  (ticket 20 in the Phase 3 tickets), temporary, and removed with the Admin's
  password box once testing is done.
  - The Admin's switch `ADMIN_PASSWORD_SIGN_IN` now turns on password sign-in
    for both Surfaces. The box is allowed and offered in the Member App
    exactly where it is in the Admin.
  - Signing in with it follows Google's path: the gate, then the Active
    Device claim for someone the gate lets in.
  - The Member App now counts a session like the Staff Guard does: Google
    everywhere, a password only where the box is allowed, and no other
    method (it fails closed).
  - Password Users are made by hand in the Supabase dashboard with "Confirm
    email" on. Testers are also added as Beta Testers. There's no sign-up
    and no password reset.

  - Seed with the mockup values (CE: 30 days ₱499, 90 days ₱1,299, 180 days

  - Price changes affect new Orders only. A Plan that's off sale is hidden
    from Members and leaves existing Access Passes unchanged.
- **Order ledger** (the state machine; the shape is a decision from the
  mockup and planning):

  ```
  Pending --paid webhook--> Paid --refund webhook--> Refunded
  Pending --failed webhook--> Failed
  Pending --24h, no outcome--> Expired
  (Paid is the only state that grants access; transitions are idempotent per PayMongo event id)
  ```

  Revised 2026-11-24 (Phase 4 planning decisions below): Failed → Paid (a
  retry on the same PayMongo page) and Expired → Paid (a late payment) are
  allowed. Paid → Refunded only on a full refund; a partial refund leaves the
  Order Paid with an amount refunded. Paid and Refunded never move back.

  - **Paid:** extend the Member's Access Pass for that Exam to
    max(current end, now) + the Plan's duration. A Course Order grants Course
    Ownership.
  - **Refunded:** subtract that Order's days from the Access Pass end (only
    that Order's days when purchases are stacked). If the end is now in the
    past, the Pass expires immediately. A Course refund removes Course
    Ownership.
  - Every grant, extension and reduction is logged in Staff Activity (system
    actor for webhooks).

- **One Access Pass per Member per Exam,** carrying its end date. The Billing
  page shows the history of contributing Orders and grants.
- **PayMongo integration** sits behind a payments interface (a real client
  and a fake for tests):
  - a hosted Checkout Session with QR Ph, GCash, Maya and card;
  - a webhook endpoint that verifies the PayMongo signature, rejects invalid
    payloads, and handles events idempotently;
  - an on-demand status check, the Owner's "Check status with PayMongo";
  - a scheduled job that expires Pending Orders after 24 hours.
- **Return flow.** After checkout the Member lands on the Pending screen,
  which polls the Order. Paid shows the success screen. Failed shows
  method-specific copy and alternatives. After about 2 minutes it shows a
  "still waiting" fallback pointing to Support.
- **References:** sequential `AYC-#####` per Order, plus the PayMongo
  reference.
- **Payment Acknowledgement.** A printable page per Paid Order, labelled "This
  is not an official invoice", with the reference, the Plan and access
  period, the amount, the method and the PayMongo reference. It has a "Request
  official invoice" action, which records the request for Owners. Official BIR
  invoices are issued outside the app. Prices are VAT-inclusive if AYC is
  VAT-registered.
- **Orders Admin** (Owner only): a list with search, filters and stats; CSV
  export of the filtered set; Order detail with timeline, internal note and
  PayMongo deep link; and the Staff Activity list for Owners.
- **Renewal nudges:** an in-app ending-soon banner from 7 days before the
  Access Pass end, comparing it with the Exam Date. A reminder email is added
  once a sending domain exists.
- **No auto-renewal, ever.** No card storage. Refunds are performed only in
  the PayMongo dashboard.

## Testing Decisions

- **What makes a good test here:** it exercises external behaviour through a
  seam's public contract (inputs to observable outputs) and never asserts on
  internals, query shapes or component structure. Each user-visible rule gets
  a **happy-path** test and its **sad paths**: invalid input, empty states,
  permission denial, expiry, replays and boundary dates. Tests read as
  examples of the rule they protect.
- **Seam 1, Catalogue Service Seam** (Phases 1–2): Vitest behaviour tests
  against the seam.
  - Happy paths: filtering by Exam, format, query and max price; each sort
    order; best-seller ranking by units sold; price labels (single, range,
    variations).
  - Sad paths:
    - Coming Soon exclusions in counts, sorts, the price filter and the
      ratings table, and never "₱0"
    - the store-button fallback (Listing → shop page → hidden)
    - an unknown slug or Exam code returns "not found"
    - an empty filter result
  - Also: the Home review selection, "as of" dates, and the computed About
    stats.
  - A selector test (mock vs database) mirrors the Customers selector test.
    The pure row-mapper tests mirror the Customers database mapper test.
- **Seam 2, Access decision** (Phases 3–5): pure Vitest tests. They cover:
  - Free Preview while signed in with no Pass
  - an active Pass for the same Exam vs another Exam
  - a Pass ending today, before and after midnight Manila time
  - an expired Pass
  - a separately sold Course, owned vs not owned
  - a replaced Active Device
  - active Staff with no Pass opening Visible content (allowed) and Hidden
    or Draft content (unavailable); removed Staff treated as a Member
    (changed on 2026-11-17)
  - the beta gate: switch off with Staff, a Beta Tester, a removed tester
    and anyone else ("not open yet"); switch on with anyone
  - no device token ("Use the Member App on this device")
  - Hidden or Unpublished content ("unavailable")
- **Seam 3, Progress calculator** (Phase 3): pure Vitest tests over
  current-state record fixtures. They cover:
  - re-marking a card (the latest wins)
  - Best Score vs latest attempt
  - Topics missing a deck, quiz or PDF
  - the Weak Topic thresholds (exactly the passing score; exactly 5 "again"
    marks)
  - streaks across Asia/Manila midnight, a gap day and the best streak
  - an empty history
  - Hidden or Draft content left out of the denominators
- **Phase 3 seams (decided 2026-11-17).**
  - **Member seam** (new Service Seam, mirroring the Catalogue seam). It has
    a sample-data arm and a database arm, and covers everything the Member
    App reads and writes for the signed-in Member: the beta gate, consent and
    re-consent, Target Exams (add, edit date, remove, the restore on re-add,
    grant-added), the Active Device (claim, switch log, replaced), content
    reads through the access decision, Card Marks, quiz submits graded on
    the server, Reviewer pages viewed, resume, notices and Privacy Requests.
    One behaviour suite runs against both arms, the database arm in CI. Sad
    paths include another Member's records never being readable, correct
    answers absent from question reads, removing the last Target Exam
    refused, and every protected read refused for a replaced device.
  - **Admin seam, extended:** Topics (add, rename, reorder, delete when
    empty), decks and cards, quizzes and questions, Reviewer PDFs (place,
    publish, blocked without a Topic or while empty), import confirm (Replace
    vs Add), Beta Testers, Users and the Member detail page, grant, extend
    and end access, and Privacy Request export and delete. These run against
    both arms with the same conventions as Phase 2: validation errors, the
    conflict check, Staff Activity, cache tags, and Owner-only actions
    refused for Editors on the server.
  - **Pure modules:** the access decision (Seam 2), the progress calculator
    (Seam 3), the import planner (each row error, formula errors naming the
    cell, a Topic created after a rename, Replace keeping Card Marks and
    Best Scores) and the consent version check.
  - **Page images:** the PDF job and the watermark stamper sit behind a small
    interface with fakes for the seam tests. Real tests run on tiny fixture
    PDFs: a 3-page PDF renders 3 pages, a password-protected or corrupt file
    fails with the right reason, a batch boundary resumes without
    duplicating or skipping pages, a stale heartbeat restarts, and a stamped
    page differs from the clean page and carries the Member's text region.
- **Seam 4, Order ledger** (Phase 4): Vitest tests driven by fixture PayMongo
  webhook payloads against the payments fake. They cover:
  - a paid webhook granting a Pass
  - stacking onto an active Pass
  - buying after expiry
  - an invalid signature rejected with no state change
  - a duplicate event applied once
  - out-of-order events
  - Pending expiring at 24 hours
  - a failed payment
  - a refund of a stacked Order removing only its days
  - a refund that ends the Pass immediately
  - a Course Order and its refund (moved to Phase 5 on 2026-11-24)
- **Phase 4 seams (decided 2026-11-24).**
  - **Payments client** (new, small): one interface for PayMongo that starts
    a Checkout Session for an Order, reads a checkout's status, and verifies
    a webhook's signature and reads its event. It has a real client and a
    fake, following the existing auth-admin interface and its fake. The fake
    also drives sample mode's "Simulate paid" and "Simulate failed".
  - **Order ledger** (pure, Seam 4): every transition, including Failed →
    Paid, Expired → Paid ("paid after expiry") and the 24-hour read-time
    expiry; stacking; full vs partial refunds and partials adding up to
    full; the unused granted days behind "End granted access" (the 1 Jan /
    10 Jan / 15 Jan example, a pass with no paid days, one with no granted
    days); and the sales figures (Manila day and month boundaries, refunds on
    their own day, Test Orders left out).
  - **Member seam, extended** (both arms, the database arm in CI): Plans for
    an Exam (pills, Target Exams first, nothing on sale hidden), Pay making a
    Pending Order with the price fixed, the Target Exam added on Paid, the
    Order poll and status check, Billing, the Payment Acknowledgement, the
    Invoice Request (one per Order), the renewal banner and its dismissal
    until the next Manila day. Sad paths include another Member's Order never
    being readable, Pay refused without a key, and Pay refused for an Exam
    with nothing on sale.
  - **Admin seam, extended** (both arms): Plans (one Best value, one on sale
    per duration, delete only without Orders), Orders (search, filters, test
    toggle, CSV columns, detail, internal note, "Check status", "Mark invoice
    sent"), business details in Settings, the sales figures, "End granted
    access", account deletion keeping detached Orders and refused while one
    is Pending, and the Staff Activity list. Owner-only actions are refused
    for Editors on the server.
  - **The webhook route:** a thin route test with fixture PayMongo payloads
    against the fake: a bad signature rejected with no change, a duplicate
    event applied once, events out of order, and a payment for a deleted
    account recorded with no grant.
- **Phase 5 seams (decided 2026-11-28).**
  - **Video service** (new, small): one interface for VdoCipher that gets

    uploads.
  - **Access decision** (pure, Seam 2, extended): a Free Preview Lesson with
    no Pass; an included Course's Lesson with an active Pass for its Exam, for
    another Exam, and with an ended Pass ("expired"); a separately sold

## Out of Scope

- **Any link between physical and digital.** Buying a Product grants no
  Member App access. No Redemption Codes, no Marketplace order linking, no
  discounts for physical buyers. (A possible later feature.)
- **Selling physical Products on the Website.** No cart and no on-site
  checkout. Physical sales stay on the Marketplaces.
- **Marketplace API sync or scraping** of ratings, units sold, stock or Reviews.
- **Stock or inventory tracking.**
- **Auto-renewal,** stored cards, and in-app refunds.
- **Partial Plans** (per-content-type unlocks) and per-item "Included in" lists
  (ADR 0010).
- **Registering the app as a BIR invoicing system.** The app issues Payment
  Acknowledgements only.
- **Dark mode.**
- **Cookie consent banner;** identified analytics on the Website.
- **Waitlist or email capture** before the Member App launch.
- **Transactional email** (invites, receipts, reminders, grant notices) until
  a verified sending domain exists. In-app notices only.
- **Offline study,** native mobile apps and push notifications.
- **Spaced-repetition scheduling** beyond Got it / Review again.
- **Automatic lockout** for device switching.
- **Customer-submitted Reviews,** or moderation queues.
- **An editable CMS for the text of Home, Products, FAQ and Reviews, and for
  the menu, footer and legal** (developer-maintained), and for the Exam
  page's compare table, "Try a card" and section headings. About's text and
  photos, and every Site page's SEO, became editable on 2026-11-05 (Phase 2:
  Site pages).
- **Admin screens for Staff Activity or denied sign-ins** (Phase 4).
- **Localisation.** The UI is English with AYC's existing Taglish touches.
- **Phase 3 exclusions (decided 2026-11-17):**
  - identified analytics or PostHog person profiles in the Member App
  - a content-usage report in the Admin (the Member detail page has each
    Member's progress summary)
  - a notification centre (notices are single dismissible banners)
  - a study event log or replaying it
  - stored stamped Reviewer PDF pages or CDN caching of them
  - automatic emailing of data copies or deletion confirmations
  - expiry or roles for Beta Testers
  - grants applied automatically when a tester is added
- **Phase 4 exclusions (decided 2026-11-24):**
  - selling Courses separately (Phase 5 builds it on Phase 4's Orders)
  - promo codes and discounts
  - more than one item per Order, or a cart
  - email reminders and our own receipt email (PayMongo's receipt is used)
  - automatic official invoices
  - proportional access changes for partial refunds
  - a separate switch for payments (the PayMongo key decides)
- **Phase 5 exclusions (decided 2026-11-26):**
  - Courses on the public Website
  - resumable uploads, and importing a video from a link (Google Drive,
    Dropbox)
  - captions, downloads or offline viewing
  - forced Lesson order (any Lesson can be opened)
  - Lessons in the progress % (they show as counts)
  - email about Courses (it waits for a sending domain)
  - proportional ownership changes for partial refunds
  - a separate switch for video (the VdoCipher key decides)
- **Multi-tenancy** of any kind (ADR 0008).
