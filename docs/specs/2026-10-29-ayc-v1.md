## Problem Statement

Ace Your Course (AYC) is a small Iloilo City company, run by people who passed
their own board exams. It makes physical reviewers (formula flash cards, A3
posters, A5 notebooks and bundles) for Philippine licensure Exams, and sells
them only on Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop.

- **No home of its own.** A reviewee searching "CELE reviewer" finds
  Marketplace listings and copycat shops, not AYC. There is nowhere that shows
  the whole catalogue by Exam, explains which format suits which way of
  studying, collects the honest reviews, answers the common questions
  (shipping, SPayLater, "is this the original?"), or proves the products are
  the genuine, IPOPHIL-registered ones.
- **Nothing digital to sell.** AYC already has the content (thousands of
  formula cards, quizzes and reviewers), but reviewees who study on their
  phones can't buy any of it.
- **Staff can't run anything themselves.** Keeping a site current, publishing
  digital content and handling digital sales all need tools that don't exist
  yet.

## Solution

One Next.js application with three Surfaces:

- **The Website** is the public catalogue and brand home.
  - Every Product AYC sells, organised by Exam, with format guidance, Shopee
    figures, hand-curated Reviews, an FAQ, an About story, an authenticity
    guide and legal pages.
  - It sells nothing itself. Every buy button opens the Product's Marketplace
    Listing.
  - Once the Member App launches, the Website also sends visitors there.
- **The Admin** is the staff-only Surface.
  - Owners and Editors manage Website content without a developer: Products
    and their Listings, Reviews, FAQs, Exam page sections, the promo banner and
    Settings.
  - They also manage Member App content: Reviewer PDFs, flashcards and quizzes
    (by hand or by spreadsheet import), and later Courses.
  - Owners also run digital sales: Plans, Orders, Members and Access Pass
    grants.
- **The Member App** is a signed-in study app.
  - A reviewee signs in with Google, adds the Exams they're preparing for (with
    a countdown to each Exam Date), and studies the Library one Topic at a
    time: flashcards, scored quizzes and watermarked Reviewer PDF pages.
  - It tracks progress, Weak Topics and a Study Streak.
  - Free Preview content lets anyone try it. A paid Plan (for example "CE
    All-Access · 3 months") unlocks all of an Exam's content for a fixed
    period, paid through PayMongo, and never renews by itself.

## User Stories

### Phase 1: Website

**Discovery and home**

1. As a Visitor, I want a Home page that says what AYC is ("Remember every formula on board day") and who made it, so that I know within seconds I'm in the right place.
2. As a Visitor, I want a "Try a card" widget that flips real sample cards, so that I can see the quality before I buy.
3. As a Visitor, I want a "Find your exam" picker on Home with my Exam's Subjects and its Products sorted by units sold, so that I can jump straight to what's relevant.
4. As a Visitor, I want a Best sellers row ranked by units sold on Shopee, so that I can see what other reviewees buy.
5. As a Visitor, I want a "Which format fits how you study?" section comparing flash cards, notebooks, posters and bundles with starting prices, so that I can choose a format.
6. As a Visitor, I want to watch a one-minute video of the products, so that I can see what's inside before I order.
7. As a Visitor on mobile data, I want the hero video not to download until I tap play, so that the page loads fast and doesn't eat my data.
8. As a Visitor, I want a scrolling wall of real Reviews, including the critical ones, so that I trust they're honest.
9. As a Visitor, I want an authenticity section explaining how to spot fake copies, so that I don't buy pirated photocopies.
10. As a Visitor, I want shop-wide trust figures (rating, number of ratings, followers) with an "as of" date, so that I know they're real and current.
11. As a Visitor, I want a dismissible announcement bar that stays dismissed on my next visit, so that it doesn't nag me.
12. As a Visitor, I want a promo banner during sales ("11.11 · Any 2 reviewers, 4% off") that disappears by itself after the sale, so that I hear about deals while they're on.

**Catalogue browsing**

13. As a Visitor, I want a Products page listing every Product for every Exam, so that I can browse the whole catalogue.
14. As a Visitor, I want to filter Products by Exam, by format and by maximum price, and search by name, Exam or Subject, so that I find the right one quickly.
15. As a Visitor, I want to sort Products by best selling, price (both directions) and top rated, so that I can compare.
16. As a Visitor, I want filters reflected in the URL, so that I can share or bookmark a filtered view.
17. As a Visitor, I want a "Help me choose" two-question picker (my Exam, how I study) that recommends one Product with a reason, so that I'm not overwhelmed.
18. As a Visitor, I want an empty state with "Clear filters" when nothing matches, so that I'm never stuck.
19. As a Visitor, I want Coming Soon Products shown in their own row with a "Coming soon" badge and no price, so that I know what's planned without mistaking it for something I can buy.
20. As a Visitor, I want Coming Soon Products left out of counts, sorting, the price filter and the ratings table, so that the numbers I see are about real, buyable Products.

**Product page**

21. As a Visitor, I want a Product page with a photo gallery, Exam, format, size, paper, edition and the Subjects covered (full names), so that I know exactly what I'm getting.
22. As a Visitor, I want to see the variations and their prices (for example "MSTC only ₱400" or "all three ₱1,250"), so that I can buy per Subject on a budget.
23. As a Visitor, I want a price range shown when a Product has variations, so that I know the cheapest way in.
24. As a Visitor, I want buy buttons for Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop that open this Product's own Listing (or the shop page if it has none), so that I can buy on my preferred Marketplace.
25. As a Visitor, I want a deal badge ("Shopee: any 2 enjoy 4% off") on Products that have the deal, so that I can save.
26. As a Visitor, I want all of the Product's Reviews and its Shopee rating figures on the page, so that I can judge it.
27. As a Visitor, I want "More for the {board}" suggestions, so that I can complete my set.
28. As a Visitor, I want a friendly message on Products with no written Reviews yet, so that a new Product doesn't look broken.

**Exam pages**

29. As a Visitor, I want an Exam page for every Exam AYC sells for (CE, ME, EE, MT, CPA, LET, PhLE and the rest), so that I land on AYC when I search for my board's reviewer.
30. As a Visitor, I want the Exam page to show its board, long name, blurb, Subjects with full names, and computed stats (number of Products, units sold, average rating, starting price), so that I see AYC's coverage at a glance.
31. As a Visitor, I want every Product for the Exam, best selling first, with format filter chips and a "Which one should I get?" comparison table, so that I can choose.
32. As a Visitor, I want three sample formula cards for my Exam, so that I can see what the content looks like.
33. As a Visitor, I want the CE bundle offer on the CE page, so that I see the best-value option.
34. As a Visitor, I want Reviews from reviewees of my Exam, so that the social proof is relevant to me.
35. As a Visitor, I want switching Exam on the Exam page to change the URL, so that I can share the right page.

**Reviews, FAQ, About, Legal**

36. As a Visitor, I want a Reviews page with ratings per Product, a searchable wall, star and Exam filters, and sorting (most helpful, highest, most critical, most detailed), so that I can find reviews that address my concerns.
37. As a Visitor, I want to filter the review wall by one Product from the ratings table, so that I can read about the exact Product I'm considering.
38. As a Visitor, I want an FAQ grouped by topic, with search, popular questions and expand or collapse all, so that I get answers without messaging the shop.
39. As a Visitor, I want some FAQ answers backed by a quote from a real Review, so that the answers feel credible.
40. As a Visitor, I want an About page with AYC's story, mission and Iloilo roots, so that I trust the people behind the products.
41. As a Visitor, I want a Privacy Policy and Terms of Use that describe what the Website actually does, so that I know how my data and purchases are handled.
42. As a Visitor, I want one consistent contact email everywhere, so that I know how to reach AYC.

**Navigation, SEO and analytics**

43. As a Visitor, I want an Exams menu listing every Exam, and a footer with Marketplace, Exam, shop and social links, so that I can get anywhere from any page.
44. As a Visitor on a phone, I want a mobile menu and layouts that fit small screens, so that the Website is easy to use where I actually browse.
45. As a Visitor, I want the Website to work without asking me to accept cookies, so that I'm not interrupted.
46. As a Visitor searching Google, I want AYC's pages to have clear titles, descriptions and share images, so that I recognise them in results and in links friends send me.
47. As a Visitor, I want accessible pages (headings, contrast, keyboard use, alt text), so that I can use the Website with assistive technology.
48. As an Owner, I want to know how many Visitors click through to each Marketplace, from which Product and page, so that I can tell whether the Website drives sales.
49. As an Owner, I want Member App buttons, badges and sections hidden until I switch the Member App on, so that Visitors never hit a dead end before it launches.
50. As a Visitor, once the Member App is live, I want an "ALSO IN APP" badge on Products whose content is in the Member App, with a "See plans" link, so that I know I can study it on my phone.

## Implementation Decisions

### Architecture and cross-cutting

- **One app, three Surfaces, one Supabase project** (ADR 0008): the Website
  at the root, the Member App under `/app`, the Admin under `/admin`. Hosted on
  Vercel Pro (Singapore) with Supabase Pro (Singapore) (ADR 0012).
- **No tenancy** (ADR 0008, supersedes 0005). Every User is a Member. Staff
  is a database staff list keyed by email, with Staff Role `owner` or `editor`
  and statuses invited, active and removed (invites expire after 7 days).
  The server makes every access decision (the Staff Guard, and from Phase 3
  the Member's own records) and connects through Drizzle with full access.
  Every table has RLS on with no `anon` or `authenticated` policies, so the
  browser's publishable key reads and writes nothing (decided in Phase 2).
  The hosted project serves development and production. Tests never touch it:
  the database tests run in CI against a local Supabase (Phase 2:
  Environments).
- **Template cleanup.** These follow from ADR 0008 and Google-only sign-in.
  - Remove the template's tenancy: Organizations, Memberships, the workspace
    switcher, the Org Role and Superadmin role model, and the tenancy
    migration and policies.
  - Remove the email and password auth flows: sign-up, confirm, reset and
    update password.
  - Remove the template dashboard demo screens (Customers, Team, Role
    settings, Blank) and the theme toggle.
  - The **Catalogue seam becomes the new Reference Feature**. Agent docs and
    the glossary are updated to point to it before Customers is removed.
- **Authentication is Google OAuth only**, through Supabase, for both the
  Member App and the Admin. Authorization decisions are made on the server
  from the staff list and the Member's own records, never from

  - A Topic has a flashcard deck, a quiz and a Reviewer PDF, each optional.
    - A deck is a list of cards, each with front text, a back in formula
      notation, an optional back image and an optional note.
    - A quiz is a list of questions, each with formula-notation text, four
      choices, the correct choice, an explanation and an optional figure,
      plus a passing score (default 70%), shuffle and "show answers after"
      settings.
    - A Reviewer PDF is an ordered set of page images, with its original file
      kept privately.
  - Decks and quizzes are Hidden or Visible. Reviewer PDFs go Processing →
    Draft → Published or Unpublished, or Processing → Failed.
  - Each deck, quiz and Reviewer PDF can be flagged Free Preview.
- **Formula notation:** LaTeX-style source rendered with KaTeX (or an
  equivalent) in both the Member App and the Admin's live preview.
- **Access decision seam** (ADR 0010 and 0011). One server-side function
  decides whether a Member may open a piece of content. It is called by every
  protected read: deck, quiz questions, Reviewer PDF page image, and later
  video. The rule:

  ```
  allowed(member, content, device) =
       device is member's Active Device
    && ( content.freePreview
      || activeAccessPass(member, content.exam)      // end > now (Asia/Manila)
      || (content.course?.soldSeparately && owns(member, content.course)) )
  ```

  Its outcomes are allowed, locked (show the unlock prompt), expired (show
  renew, progress kept) and device-replaced (show Session ended).

- **Active Device** (ADR 0011).
  - A custom record per Member. A Member App sign-in sets it.
  - Every protected request compares the request's device token with it.
  - Each switch is logged with its time and a device label from the user
    agent (for example "Chrome on Android").
  - Admin sessions are exempt. Supabase's single-session setting is not used.
- **Burned-in watermark.** Reviewer PDF pages are only ever served through a
  protected page endpoint. It runs the access decision, then stamps the
  Member's email and short Member ID into the page image, and caches the
  stamped image per Member and page. Original PDFs and clean page images are
  never sent to clients. Cards and quizzes get an overlay watermark and no
  text selection.
- **PDF processing** is a background job (ADR 0012).
  1. The Admin uploads straight to private storage, up to 200 MB.
  2. A job renders the page images and records its progress, which the Admin
     shows.
  3. It ends as a Draft, or as Failed with a reason (password-protected,
     corrupt, too large).
  4. Deleting a Reviewer PDF removes its original and its page images.
- **Spreadsheet import** (cards and quizzes).
  - It is a pure validator and planner. It turns the uploaded CSV (plus an
    images zip) into a preview of the Topics to create, the cards or
    questions to add, replace or keep, and row-level errors: a missing field,
    a formula that doesn't render, a missing image, an invalid correct-answer
    letter.
  - Nothing is saved until Staff confirm.
  - Imported content arrives Hidden. Topics are created from the `topic`
    column.
  - Re-importing offers Replace or Add. Replace keeps Card Marks on cards
    whose front text is unchanged.
  - Downloadable templates are provided.
- **Target Exams.** A Member has any number of them (Exam plus Exam Date).
  The Exam Date defaults to the Exam's Official Exam Date and follows Staff
  updates until the Member edits it. The dashboard is per selected Target
  Exam. Exam pills in onboarding are selectable (a mockup bug).
- **Progress calculator** (pure functions over study events). Events are
  Card Marked, Quiz Attempt Submitted, Reviewer Page Viewed and, in Phase 5,
  Lesson Progress.
  - **Card Mark:** got or again; the latest wins.
  - **Quiz:** Best Score per quiz. The "quiz average" is the mean of Best
    Scores across the quizzes taken.
  - **Topic %:** the mean of the components present: share of cards marked
    got, Best Score %, share of Reviewer PDF pages viewed.
  - **Subject %:** the mean of its Topic %.
  - **Weak Topic:** Best Score below the passing score, or 5 or more cards
    marked again. The 3 weakest show, lowest first.
  - **Study Day:** any event on an Asia/Manila calendar day.
  - **Study Streak:** consecutive Study Days, current and best.
  - Progress is stored per Member, never deleted when access ends, and
    counted on Free Preview content too.
- **Resume state:** the last deck position, the last Reviewer PDF page (with
  zoom) and, later, the Lesson position are stored per Member.
- **Consent.**
  - Two required checkboxes, unchecked by default; "Agree" is disabled until
    both are ticked.
  - The agreed Terms and Privacy versions and a timestamp are stored.
  - A version change re-prompts the Member.
  - Legal v2.0 (the Member App sections) ships with this phase's public
    launch.
- **Privacy Requests** (data copy and deletion) are records Owners fulfil by
  hand at launch: a copy within 7 days, deletion within 30. Deletion revokes
  Access Passes with no refund, as the confirmation screen says.
- **Beta access.** The Owner-only Users list, the Member detail page, "Grant
  Access Pass" (Exam, days, reason) and "Extend access" (3, 7, 14 or 30 days)
  ship in Phase 3, all logged to Staff Activity. Members get an in-app notice;
  email waits until a sending domain exists.
- **Reviewer PDF reader:** one page at a time, zoom 75–150% in 25% steps, no
  offline caching, load-error retry, and an empty-Topic state.
- **The mockup's hardcoded CE labels** (sidebar badge, dashboard header,
  countdown label, Library heading) become per Target Exam. The unused
  mobile tab-bar code from v1 of the mockup is not built. Mobile uses the
  Menu button and drawer.

### Phase 4: Payments

- **Plans.** Each Plan has an Exam, a name, a duration of 30, 90, 180 or 365
  days (labelled 1, 3, 6 or 12 months), a price, Best value and On sale.
  - Owners manage them.
  - Seed with the mockup values (CE: 30 days ₱499, 90 days ₱1,299, 180 days
    ₱2,299; other Exams: 30 days ₱449) until the client confirms prices.
  - Price changes affect new Orders only. A Plan that's off sale is hidden
    from Members and leaves existing Access Passes unchanged.
- **Order ledger** (the state machine; the shape is a decision from the
  mockup and planning):

  ```
  Pending --paid webhook--> Paid --refund webhook--> Refunded
  Pending --failed webhook--> Failed
  Pending --24h, no outcome--> Expired
  (Paid is the only state that grants access; transitions are idempotent per PayMongo event id)
  ```

  - **Paid:** extend the Member's Access Pass for that Exam to
    max(current end, now) + the Plan's duration. A Course Order grants Course
    Ownership.
  - **Refunded:** subtract that Order's days from the Access Pass end (only
    that Order's days when purchases are stacked). If the end is now in the
    past, the Pass expires immediately. A Course refund removes Course
    Ownership.
  - Every grant, extension and reduction is logged in Staff Activity (system
    actor for webhooks).

- **One Access Pass per Member per Exam,** carrying its end date. The Billing
  page shows the history of contributing Orders and grants.
- **PayMongo integration** sits behind a payments interface (a real client
  and a fake for tests):
  - a hosted Checkout Session with QR Ph, GCash, Maya and card;
  - a webhook endpoint that verifies the PayMongo signature, rejects invalid
    payloads, and handles events idempotently;
  - an on-demand status check, the Owner's "Check status with PayMongo";
  - a scheduled job that expires Pending Orders after 24 hours.
- **Return flow.** After checkout the Member lands on the Pending screen,
  which polls the Order. Paid shows the success screen. Failed shows
  method-specific copy and alternatives. After about 2 minutes it shows a
  "still waiting" fallback pointing to Support.
- **References:** sequential `AYC-#####` per Order, plus the PayMongo
  reference.
- **Payment Acknowledgement.** A printable page per Paid Order, labelled "This
  is not an official invoice", with the reference, the Plan and access
  period, the amount, the method and the PayMongo reference. It has a "Request
  official invoice" action, which records the request for Owners. Official BIR
  invoices are issued outside the app. Prices are VAT-inclusive if AYC is
  VAT-registered.
- **Orders Admin** (Owner only): a list with search, filters and stats; CSV
  export of the filtered set; Order detail with timeline, internal note and
  PayMongo deep link; and the Staff Activity list for Owners.
- **Renewal nudges:** an in-app ending-soon banner from 7 days before the
  Access Pass end, comparing it with the Exam Date. A reminder email is added
  once a sending domain exists.
- **No auto-renewal, ever.** No card storage. Refunds are performed only in
  the PayMongo dashboard.

### Phase 5: Video Courses (to be planned)

- **Course → Module → Lesson.** A Course is either included in its Exam's
  All-Access or sold separately (Course Ownership). Each Lesson has a Free
  Preview flag. A Lesson is done at 90% watched or by "Mark complete". Members
  resume where they stopped.
- **Provider not chosen yet.** It must offer DRM (Widevine and FairPlay), a
  per-Member watermark, resumable uploads up to 4 GB and signed playback.
  Candidates: VdoCipher, Mux, Bunny Stream. Choosing one (an ADR) is the first
  task of the Phase 5 planning session.

## Testing Decisions

- **What makes a good test here:** it exercises external behaviour through a
  seam's public contract (inputs to observable outputs) and never asserts on
  internals, query shapes or component structure. Each user-visible rule gets
  a **happy-path** test and its **sad paths**: invalid input, empty states,
  permission denial, expiry, replays and boundary dates. Tests read as
  examples of the rule they protect.
- **Seam 1, Catalogue Service Seam** (Phases 1–2): Vitest behaviour tests
  against the seam.
  - Happy paths: filtering by Exam, format, query and max price; each sort
    order; best-seller ranking by units sold; price labels (single, range,
    variations).
  - Sad paths:
    - Coming Soon exclusions in counts, sorts, the price filter and the
      ratings table, and never "₱0"
    - the store-button fallback (Listing → shop page → hidden)
    - an unknown slug or Exam code returns "not found"
    - an empty filter result
  - Also: the Home review selection, "as of" dates, and the computed About
    stats.
  - A selector test (mock vs database) mirrors the Customers selector test.
    The pure row-mapper tests mirror the Customers database mapper test.
- **Seam 2, Access decision** (Phases 3–5): pure Vitest tests. They cover:
  - Free Preview while signed in with no Pass
  - an active Pass for the same Exam vs another Exam
  - a Pass ending today, before and after midnight Manila time
  - an expired Pass
  - a separately sold Course, owned vs not owned
  - a replaced Active Device
  - Staff with no Pass (no special access)
- **Seam 3, Progress calculator** (Phase 3): pure Vitest tests over event
  fixtures. They cover:
  - re-marking a card (the latest wins)
  - Best Score vs latest attempt
  - Topics missing a deck, quiz or PDF
  - the Weak Topic thresholds (exactly the passing score; exactly 5 "again"
    marks)
  - streaks across Asia/Manila midnight, a gap day and the best streak
  - an empty history
- **Seam 4, Order ledger** (Phase 4): Vitest tests driven by fixture PayMongo
  webhook payloads against the payments fake. They cover:
  - a paid webhook granting a Pass
  - stacking onto an active Pass
  - buying after expiry
  - an invalid signature rejected with no state change
  - a duplicate event applied once
  - out-of-order events
  - Pending expiring at 24 hours
  - a failed payment
  - a refund of a stacked Order removing only its days
  - a refund that ends the Pass immediately
  - a Course Order and its refund
- **Pure validators:** spreadsheet import planning (valid file; each row-error
  type; Replace vs Add keeping Card Marks), Staff Role permission checks, and
  consent version re-prompting.
- **Permission and deny-all tests.** The server decides access, so the Staff
  Role rules are tested through the Admin seam and its Server Actions: an
  Editor is refused Settings and Staff actions on the server, the last Owner
  can't be removed or demoted, nobody can remove themselves. A database test
  proves the browser keys can't read or write any table (RLS on, no
  policies). Database tests follow the Customers RLS integration test (git
  history at `aea34ea`) and skip cleanly when local Supabase isn't running.
- **Admin seam (Phase 2):** one behaviour suite per area (settings, Staff and
  Invites, Products, photos, Reviews, FAQs, Exam pages, promo) runs against
  the sample-data arm in Stage A and against both arms from Stage B, with the
  Catalogue seam's existing suite also run against the database arm. Each
  covers validation errors, the conflict check, Staff Activity records and
  the cache tags returned.
- **Seam 5, Playwright journeys.**
  - **Website:** Home → Exam → Product → Marketplace button has the right
    URL; filters update the URL; Coming Soon rendering; Member App switch off
    hides all app CTAs.
  - **Admin:** an Editor edits a Product and sees it live on the Website; an
    Editor is refused Settings; an invited email activates on sign-in; a
    non-Staff User sees the 403 screen. Admin specs change shared data, so
    they run in their own Playwright project after the Website specs, one
    worker, each resetting the sample data (Stage A) or the database seed
    (Stage B) first.
  - **Member App:** sign-in and consent → onboarding → Free Preview deck → a
    locked Topic shows the unlock prompt; the Session-ended screen.
  - **Payments:** checkout with the PayMongo fake → Pending → Paid → content
    unlocked.
  - The existing accessibility check (axe, serious and critical) extends to
    every public Website page and the Member App's main screens.
- **Existing helpers:** the SEO helper tests extend to the new sitemap (every
  Exam and Product) and to JSON-LD (no rating markup).
- **Prior art:** the Customers seam behaviour test, seam selector test,
  database mapper test and RLS integration test; the component tests using
  Testing Library; the `seo`, `authz` and `csp` helper tests; and the
  Playwright `a11y` and `auth-smoke` suites.

## Out of Scope

- **Any link between physical and digital.** Buying a Product grants no
  Member App access. No Redemption Codes, no Marketplace order linking, no
  discounts for physical buyers. (A possible later feature.)
- **Selling physical Products on the Website.** No cart and no on-site
  checkout. Physical sales stay on the Marketplaces.
- **Marketplace API sync or scraping** of ratings, units sold, stock or Reviews.
- **Stock or inventory tracking.**
- **Auto-renewal,** stored cards, and in-app refunds.
- **Partial Plans** (per-content-type unlocks) and per-item "Included in" lists
  (ADR 0010).
- **Registering the app as a BIR invoicing system.** The app issues Payment
  Acknowledgements only.
- **Dark mode.**
- **Cookie consent banner;** identified analytics on the Website.
- **Waitlist or email capture** before the Member App launch.
- **Transactional email** (invites, receipts, reminders, grant notices) until
  a verified sending domain exists. In-app notices only.
- **Offline study,** native mobile apps and push notifications.
- **Spaced-repetition scheduling** beyond Got it / Review again.
- **Automatic lockout** for device switching.
- **Customer-submitted Reviews,** or moderation queues.
- **An editable CMS for the text of Home, Products, FAQ and Reviews, and for
  the menu, footer and legal** (developer-maintained), and for the Exam
  page's compare table, "Try a card" and section headings. About's text and
  photos, and every Site page's SEO, became editable on 2026-11-05 (Phase 2:
  Site pages).
- **Admin screens for Staff Activity or denied sign-ins** (Phase 4).
- **Localisation.** The UI is English with AYC's existing Taglish touches.
- **Phase 5 implementation details,** until its planning session.
- **Multi-tenancy** of any kind (ADR 0008).
